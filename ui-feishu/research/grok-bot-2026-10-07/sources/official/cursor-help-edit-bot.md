<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-rename-a-bot"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-rename-a-bot" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-rename-a-bot" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-rename-a-bot"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I rename a Bot?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">On desktop, right-click the Bot in the sidebar and choose <strong>Rename Bot</strong>. You can also double-click the Bot&#x27;s name in the sidebar. Type the new name and press Enter. If the sidebar is collapsed to icons, expand it first.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">You can also change the name in <strong>Bot settings</strong>. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="#how-do-i-change-what-a-bot-does">How do I change what a Bot does?</a></p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">On the phone, tap the Bot&#x27;s name at the top of the chat to open its profile. Then tap <strong>Edit name</strong>.</p>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">On desktop:</p>
<ol class="mb-4 prose-ol-markers" style="list-style-type:decimal;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Open the chat with the Bot.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Click the Bot&#x27;s name at the top of the chat to open its details.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Click the Bot&#x27;s picture.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Choose a tab:<!-- -->
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Bot</strong> changes the look of the Bot&#x27;s character.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Generate</strong> makes a picture from a short description. Describe the picture, choose <strong>Generate</strong>, then choose <strong>Set avatar</strong>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Upload</strong> uses your own image. Drag in an image or choose <strong>Browse files</strong>, adjust it, then choose <strong>Set avatar</strong>. The image must be smaller than 25 MB.</li>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">You can also change the name in <strong>Bot settings</strong>.
