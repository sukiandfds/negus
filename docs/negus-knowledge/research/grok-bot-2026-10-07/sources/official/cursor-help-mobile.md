<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">There is no setting that stays quiet only while the desktop app is open.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To stop alerts for one Bot, open that Bot and turn off <strong>Notifications</strong>. The note is <strong>Get notified when this Bot finishes or needs input</strong>. The same switch is in <strong>Bot settings</strong> on desktop. Off means that Bot does not notify you when it finishes or needs input, including on your phone.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To stop phone alerts for every Bot, open <strong>Settings</strong> on the phone and turn off <strong>Notifications</strong>. If the phone says <strong>Notifications are off</strong>, choose <strong>Open Settings</strong> and allow notifications for Grok Bot there only if you want them back.</p>
