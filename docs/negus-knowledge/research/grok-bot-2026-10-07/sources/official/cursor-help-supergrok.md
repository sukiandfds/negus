<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="what-do-i-need-before-linking-supergrok"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#what-do-i-need-before-linking-supergrok" data-heading-base-id="what-do-i-need-before-linking-supergrok" aria-label="Copy link to what-do-i-need-before-linking-supergrok"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">What do I need before linking SuperGrok?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">You need an active <strong>individual SuperGrok</strong>, <strong>SuperGrok Plus</strong>, <strong>SuperGrok Heavy</strong>, or <strong>X Premium+</strong> subscription on the Grok or X account you will link, and a Cursor or Grok Bot account to receive the usage grant.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Linking pairs one Cursor account with one Grok or X account. The link itself is not a Cursor payment. Sign in with the Cursor account that should hold the usage grant, because a SuperGrok or X Premium+ link <strong>can&#x27;t be unlinked or moved</strong> to a different Cursor account once created.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="what-do-i-get-after-linking-supergrok"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#what-do-i-get-after-linking-supergrok" data-heading-base-id="what-do-i-get-after-linking-supergrok" aria-label="Copy link to what-do-i-get-after-linking-supergrok"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">What do I get after linking SuperGrok?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Linking is a <strong>usage grant</strong>, not a Cursor plan. It grants Grok Bot usage on the linked Cursor account while your SuperGrok or X Premium+ subscription stays active.</p>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>SuperGrok Heavy</strong> grants the highest linked Grok Bot usage.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>SuperGrok Plus</strong> grants generous linked Grok Bot usage, below the Heavy tier.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>SuperGrok</strong> grants linked Grok Bot usage, below the Plus tier.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>X Premium+</strong> grants linked Grok Bot usage, below SuperGrok Plus.</li>
</ul>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Cursor re-checks your SuperGrok or X Premium+ status at each renewal.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Upgrading or downgrading SuperGrok after you have already linked can take up to 24 hours to show on <strong>Weekly usage</strong>. You do not need to link again. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/plans#what-if-i-upgrade-supergrok-after-i-already-linked">What if I upgrade SuperGrok after I already linked?</a>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">The grant does not stack with a Cursor plan. If you are on <strong>Pro+</strong>, linking <strong>SuperGrok Plus</strong> does not add usage. Linking <strong>SuperGrok</strong>, <strong>SuperGrok Plus</strong>, <strong>SuperGrok Heavy</strong>, or <strong>X Premium+</strong> on top of Pro, Pro+, Ultra, or a self-serve Teams plan does not add usage either. You can still upgrade within Cursor plans, such as <strong>Pro+</strong> to <strong>Ultra</strong>. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/plans#do-cursor-and-supergrok-plans-stack">Do Cursor and SuperGrok plans stack?</a>.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="does-linking-supergrok-change-my-cursor-plan"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#does-linking-supergrok-change-my-cursor-plan" data-heading-base-id="does-linking-supergrok-change-my-cursor-plan" aria-label="Copy link to does-linking-supergrok-change-my-cursor-plan"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">Does linking SuperGrok change my Cursor plan?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">No. Your existing Cursor plan stays in place. Linking SuperGrok or X Premium+ doesn&#x27;t cancel a Pro, Pro+, Ultra, or Teams subscription, and it doesn&#x27;t replace them with another Cursor plan. If you had no Cursor plan before linking, you still don&#x27;t, and Grok Bot usage comes from the SuperGrok or X Premium+ grant alone.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">If you have both a paid Cursor plan that includes Grok Bot and a SuperGrok or X Premium+ link, both stay on the same Cursor account, but they do not stack. Linking <strong>SuperGrok Plus</strong> while you are on <strong>Pro+</strong> does not add Grok Bot usage. Purchasing <strong>Cursor Ultra</strong> while <strong>SuperGrok Heavy</strong> is linked does not grant more included usage either. You can still move from <strong>Pro+</strong> to <strong>Ultra</strong> on the Cursor plan itself. That upgrade changes the Cursor grant. It does not combine with SuperGrok.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Your Cursor plan continues to bill and renew independently of the link. Neither subscription cancels the other. Linking SuperGrok or X Premium+ does not swap you to Ultra, cancel a Cursor plan, or add a second Grok Bot meter.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">If you need more usage after the included grant runs out, enable <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/account-and-billing/overages">on-demand usage</a>. That is the path for extra usage. Linking SuperGrok or X Premium+ on top of a Cursor plan is not. You can still upgrade the Cursor plan, such as <strong>Pro+</strong> to <strong>Ultra</strong>.</p>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Link your Grok or X account to grant Grok Bot usage on your Cursor account. Individual SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus, SuperGrok Heavy, and X Premium+ qualify.</p>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">You need an active <strong>individual SuperGrok</strong>, <strong>SuperGrok Plus</strong>, <strong>SuperGrok Heavy</strong>, or <strong>X Premium+</strong> subscription on the Grok or X account you will link, and a Cursor or Grok Bot account to receive the usage grant.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Linking pairs one Cursor account with one Grok or X account. The link itself is not a Cursor payment. Sign in with the Cursor account that should hold the usage grant, because a SuperGrok or X Premium+ link <strong>can&#x27;t be unlinked or moved</strong> to a different Cursor account once created.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="what-do-i-get-after-linking-supergrok"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#what-do-i-get-after-linking-supergrok" data-heading-base-id="what-do-i-get-after-linking-supergrok" aria-label="Copy link to what-do-i-get-after-linking-supergrok"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">What do I get after linking SuperGrok?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Linking is a <strong>usage grant</strong>, not a Cursor plan. It grants Grok Bot usage on the linked Cursor account while your SuperGrok or X Premium+ subscription stays active.</p>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>SuperGrok Heavy</strong> grants the highest linked Grok Bot usage.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>SuperGrok Plus</strong> grants generous linked Grok Bot usage, below the Heavy tier.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>SuperGrok</strong> grants linked Grok Bot usage, below the Plus tier.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>X Premium+</strong> grants linked Grok Bot usage, below SuperGrok Plus.</li>
</ul>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Cursor re-checks your SuperGrok or X Premium+ status at each renewal.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Upgrading or downgrading SuperGrok after you have already linked can take up to 24 hours to show on <strong>Weekly usage</strong>. You do not need to link again. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/plans#what-if-i-upgrade-supergrok-after-i-already-linked">What if I upgrade SuperGrok after I already linked?</a>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">The grant does not stack with a Cursor plan. If you are on <strong>Pro+</strong>, linking <strong>SuperGrok Plus</strong> does not add usage. Linking <strong>SuperGrok</strong>, <strong>SuperGrok Plus</strong>, <strong>SuperGrok Heavy</strong>, or <strong>X Premium+</strong> on top of Pro, Pro+, Ultra, or a self-serve Teams plan does not add usage either. You can still upgrade within Cursor plans, such as <strong>Pro+</strong> to <strong>Ultra</strong>. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/plans#do-cursor-and-supergrok-plans-stack">Do Cursor and SuperGrok plans stack?</a>.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="does-linking-supergrok-change-my-cursor-plan"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#does-linking-supergrok-change-my-cursor-plan" data-heading-base-id="does-linking-supergrok-change-my-cursor-plan" aria-label="Copy link to does-linking-supergrok-change-my-cursor-plan"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">Does linking SuperGrok change my Cursor plan?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">No. Your existing Cursor plan stays in place. Linking SuperGrok or X Premium+ doesn&#x27;t cancel a Pro, Pro+, Ultra, or Teams subscription, and it doesn&#x27;t replace them with another Cursor plan. If you had no Cursor plan before linking, you still don&#x27;t, and Grok Bot usage comes from the SuperGrok or X Premium+ grant alone.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">If you have both a paid Cursor plan that includes Grok Bot and a SuperGrok or X Premium+ link, both stay on the same Cursor account, but they do not stack. Linking <strong>SuperGrok Plus</strong> while you are on <strong>Pro+</strong> does not add Grok Bot usage. Purchasing <strong>Cursor Ultra</strong> while <strong>SuperGrok Heavy</strong> is linked does not grant more included usage either. You can still move from <strong>Pro+</strong> to <strong>Ultra</strong> on the Cursor plan itself. That upgrade changes the Cursor grant. It does not combine with SuperGrok.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Your Cursor plan continues to bill and renew independently of the link. Neither subscription cancels the other. Linking SuperGrok or X Premium+ does not swap you to Ultra, cancel a Cursor plan, or add a second Grok Bot meter.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">If you need more usage after the included grant runs out, enable <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/account-and-billing/overages">on-demand usage</a>. That is the path for extra usage. Linking SuperGrok or X Premium+ on top of a Cursor plan is not. You can still upgrade the Cursor plan, such as <strong>Pro+</strong> to <strong>Ultra</strong>.</p>
