<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="who-can-use-team-bots"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#who-can-use-team-bots" data-heading-base-id="who-can-use-team-bots" aria-label="Copy link to who-can-use-team-bots"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">Who can use Team Bots?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Team Bots are for members of a Cursor team who have Grok Bot. If you don&#x27;t see <strong>Create new Team Bot</strong> after you choose <strong>New</strong> in the sidebar, Team Bots aren&#x27;t on for your team yet. Opening a Team Bot link then shows <strong>Team Bots Not Available</strong>.</p>

<ol class="mb-4 prose-ol-markers" style="list-style-type:decimal;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Choose <strong>New</strong> in the sidebar, then <strong>Create new Team Bot</strong>. Grok Bot creates a Bot named <strong>New team bot</strong> and opens its chat.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Tell the Bot what it should help the team with. A sentence is enough.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Follow its setup steps. It asks for plugins, then secrets, then files. Only add keys and files you&#x27;re happy for the whole team to use.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Give the Bot a name and description. Click the Bot&#x27;s name at the top of the chat, then choose <strong>Edit details</strong>. Teammates can&#x27;t see a Team Bot that still has the default name and no description.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Publish it. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="#how-do-i-publish-a-team-bot">How do I publish a Team Bot?</a></li>
</ol>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-turn-a-bot-i-already-have-into-a-team-bot"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-turn-a-bot-i-already-have-into-a-team-bot" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-turn-a-bot-i-already-have-into-a-team-bot" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-turn-a-bot-i-already-have-into-a-team-bot"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I turn a Bot I already have into a Team Bot?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Open the Bot&#x27;s <strong>Share</strong> menu and choose <strong>Publish to Team</strong>. The Bot makes a separate team copy of itself and walks you through what to bring:</p>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Secrets. Only bring keys you&#x27;re happy for the whole team to use.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Memories. The Bot shows you which of its memories the team should have.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Routines. You can move them to the team copy or keep them on your personal Bot.</li>
</ul>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">The personal Bot keeps working, and its chats don&#x27;t move to the team copy. The team copy appears in your sidebar, hidden from teammates until you publish it.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-publish-a-team-bot"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-publish-a-team-bot" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-publish-a-team-bot" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-publish-a-team-bot"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I publish a Team Bot?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">When the Bot is ready, it shows a card that says it&#x27;s ready for your team. Choose <strong>Publish to team</strong>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">After you publish, teammates can find the Bot by choosing <strong>New</strong>, then <strong>Team Bots</strong>. The card also has a button to copy the Bot&#x27;s link, so you can send it to people.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To stop sharing, click the Bot&#x27;s name at the top of the chat and choose <strong>Unpublish</strong>. Teammates lose access until you publish it again.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-teammates-find-a-team-bot"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-teammates-find-a-team-bot" data-heading-base-id="how-do-teammates-find-a-team-bot" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-teammates-find-a-team-bot"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do teammates find a Team Bot?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Choose <strong>New</strong> in the sidebar, then <strong>Team Bots</strong>. Search or scroll the list. Each Bot shows who made it.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">In the regular <strong>New</strong> search, type at least 3 characters of the Bot&#x27;s name.</li>

</ul>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To copy a published Team Bot&#x27;s link, right-click it in the sidebar and choose <strong>Copy link</strong>. A Team Bot link opens the same shared Bot. It&#x27;s different from <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/docs/grok-bot/work#share-a-bot">sharing a Bot template</a>, which gives each person their own copy.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="can-the-owner-see-my-chats-with-a-team-bot"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#can-the-owner-see-my-chats-with-a-team-bot" data-heading-base-id="can-the-owner-see-my-chats-with-a-team-bot" aria-label="Copy link to can-the-owner-see-my-chats-with-a-team-bot"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">Can the owner see my chats with a Team Bot?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">No. Each person&#x27;s chat with a Team Bot is private. The owner can&#x27;t read your chats, and you can&#x27;t read the owner&#x27;s or anyone else&#x27;s.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">The Bot can save things it learns for the whole team, and it tells you when it does. It doesn&#x27;t save anything about you personally for the team. What it remembers about working with you stays between you and the Bot.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="whose-plugins-and-secrets-does-a-team-bot-use"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#whose-plugins-and-secrets-does-a-team-bot-use" data-heading-base-id="whose-plugins-and-secrets-does-a-team-bot-use" aria-label="Copy link to whose-plugins-and-secrets-does-a-team-bot-use"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">Whose plugins and secrets does a Team Bot use?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">A Team Bot uses the plugins and secrets its owner added. Those work the same for everyone who chats with it.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">In your own chat with a Team Bot, it can also use your plugins. It asks before it uses one of your personal accounts. The card says <strong>Allow</strong>, then the plugin name:</p>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Allow once</strong> lets it use that account this time.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Always allow</strong> remembers your answer. Its menu has <strong>Always allow for this Bot</strong> and <strong>Always allow for all Team Bots</strong>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Skip</strong> means it doesn&#x27;t use that account this time.</li>
</ul>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">In Slack channels, threads, and group chats, a Team Bot uses only its owner&#x27;s plugins, and asks before it uses your accounts.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">A connected account always acts as the person the Bot is answering. One teammate&#x27;s connection is never used for someone else.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To make every Team Bot ask again, open <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>General</strong> &gt; <strong>Team Bots</strong> and choose <strong>Clear</strong> next to <strong>Clear connector preferences</strong>.</p>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">In your own chat with a teammate&#x27;s Team Bot, the Bot usually works on your Grok Bot computer. That means it can use the files there and the sites you&#x27;re signed in to there. Sometimes it uses a separate computer just for that conversation, for example when your privacy mode is stricter than the owner&#x27;s.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">In Slack channels, threads, and group chats, a Team Bot uses one shared computer of its own.</p>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">A Team Bot asks for approval only in its owner&#x27;s own chat with it. In teammates&#x27; chats, in Slack, and in group chats, nobody who can answer an approval card is there. So the Bot works within the permissions its owner set up, without stopping to ask. It still asks before it uses your personal accounts.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">If your Enterprise admin enforces Auto-review, Auto-review still checks actions in those chats. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/docs/grok-bot/teams#enforce-auto-review">Grok Bot for Teams and Enterprise</a>.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-add-a-team-bot-to-slack"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-add-a-team-bot-to-slack" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-add-a-team-bot-to-slack" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-add-a-team-bot-to-slack"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I add a Team Bot to Slack?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">The owner adds it, after publishing:</p>
<ol class="mb-4 prose-ol-markers" style="list-style-type:decimal;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Click the Bot&#x27;s name at the top of the chat.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Find <strong>Bring to your team&#x27;s Slack</strong> and choose <strong>Connect</strong>. Before the Bot is published, this says <strong>Connect after publishing</strong>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">If it says <strong>Awaiting admin approval</strong>, a Slack workspace admin needs to approve the app.</li>
</ol>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Once it says <strong>Connected</strong>, teammates can message the Bot directly or mention it in channels.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Each teammate needs to link their Slack account first. Open <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>General</strong> &gt; <strong>Team Bots</strong> and choose <strong>Link</strong>. If you message the Bot before you link, it replies with steps to link your account.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-edit-unpublish-or-delete-a-team-bot"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-edit-unpublish-or-delete-a-team-bot" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-edit-unpublish-or-delete-a-team-bot" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-edit-unpublish-or-delete-a-team-bot"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I edit, unpublish, or delete a Team Bot?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Only the owner can change a Team Bot. Click the Bot&#x27;s name at the top of the chat to open its details:</p>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Plugins</strong>, <strong>Secrets</strong>, <strong>Skills</strong>, and <strong>Files</strong> hold what the Bot uses for everyone.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Edit details</strong> changes the name and description.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Unpublish</strong> hides the Bot from teammates until you publish it again.</li>
</ul>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To rename it, you can also right-click the Bot in the sidebar and choose <strong>Rename Bot</strong>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To delete it, right-click the Bot in the sidebar and choose <strong>Delete</strong>. This deletes the Bot for your whole team. Teammates lose it, along with their chats and routines on it. It can&#x27;t be undone.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-remove-a-team-bot-from-my-sidebar"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-remove-a-team-bot-from-my-sidebar" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-remove-a-team-bot-from-my-sidebar" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-remove-a-team-bot-from-my-sidebar"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I remove a Team Bot from my sidebar?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Right-click the Bot and choose <strong>Hide from sidebar</strong>. To add it back, choose <strong>New</strong>, then <strong>Team Bots</strong>, and pick it again.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">If your admin added the Bot to your sidebar for you, <strong>Hide from sidebar</strong> isn&#x27;t in its menu, and you can&#x27;t remove it.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="can-i-use-team-bots-on-my-phone"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#can-i-use-team-bots-on-my-phone" data-heading-base-id="can-i-use-team-bots-on-my-phone" aria-label="Copy link to can-i-use-team-bots-on-my-phone"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">Can I use Team Bots on my phone?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Yes, for chatting. Team Bots you&#x27;ve added appear in the phone app. You can chat with them and answer their <strong>Allow</strong> cards. Create, publish, edit, and connect Team Bots to Slack on desktop.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">You can&#x27;t start a voice call with a teammate&#x27;s Team Bot.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="can-a-team-bot-join-a-group-chat"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#can-a-team-bot-join-a-group-chat" data-heading-base-id="can-a-team-bot-join-a-group-chat" aria-label="Copy link to can-a-team-bot-join-a-group-chat"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">Can a Team Bot join a group chat?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Open the Bot&#x27;s <strong>Share</strong> menu and choose <strong>Publish to Team</strong>. The Bot makes a separate team copy of itself and walks you through what to bring:</p>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Secrets. Only bring keys you&#x27;re happy for the whole team to use.</li>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">The personal Bot keeps working, and its chats don&#x27;t move to the team copy. The team copy appears in your sidebar, hidden from teammates until you publish it.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-publish-a-team-bot"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-publish-a-team-bot" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-publish-a-team-bot" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-publish-a-team-bot"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I publish a Team Bot?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">When the Bot is ready, it shows a card that says it&#x27;s ready for your team. Choose <strong>Publish to team</strong>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">After you publish, teammates can find the Bot by choosing <strong>New</strong>, then <strong>Team Bots</strong>. The card also has a button to copy the Bot&#x27;s link, so you can send it to people.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To stop sharing, click the Bot&#x27;s name at the top of the chat and choose <strong>Unpublish</strong>. Teammates lose access until you publish it again.</p>

<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Choose <strong>New</strong> in the sidebar, then <strong>Team Bots</strong>. Search or scroll the list. Each Bot shows who made it.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">In the regular <strong>New</strong> search, type at least 3 characters of the Bot&#x27;s name.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Open a link a teammate sent you, then choose <strong>Start a chat</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To copy a published Team Bot&#x27;s link, right-click it in the sidebar and choose <strong>Copy link</strong>. A Team Bot link opens the same shared Bot. It&#x27;s different from <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/docs/grok-bot/work#share-a-bot">sharing a Bot template</a>, which gives each person their own copy.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="can-the-owner-see-my-chats-with-a-team-bot"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#can-the-owner-see-my-chats-with-a-team-bot" data-heading-base-id="can-the-owner-see-my-chats-with-a-team-bot" aria-label="Copy link to can-the-owner-see-my-chats-with-a-team-bot"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">Can the owner see my chats with a Team Bot?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">No. Each person&#x27;s chat with a Team Bot is private. The owner can&#x27;t read your chats, and you can&#x27;t read the owner&#x27;s or anyone else&#x27;s.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">The Bot can save things it learns for the whole team, and it tells you when it does. It doesn&#x27;t save anything about you personally for the team. What it remembers about working with you stays between you and the Bot.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="whose-plugins-and-secrets-does-a-team-bot-use"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#whose-plugins-and-secrets-does-a-team-bot-use" data-heading-base-id="whose-plugins-and-secrets-does-a-team-bot-use" aria-label="Copy link to whose-plugins-and-secrets-does-a-team-bot-use"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">Whose plugins and secrets does a Team Bot use?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">A Team Bot uses the plugins and secrets its owner added. Those work the same for everyone who chats with it.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">In your own chat with a Team Bot, it can also use your plugins. It asks before it uses one of your personal accounts. The card says <strong>Allow</strong>, then the plugin name:</p>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Allow once</strong> lets it use that account this time.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Always allow</strong> remembers your answer. Its menu has <strong>Always allow for this Bot</strong> and <strong>Always allow for all Team Bots</strong>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Skip</strong> means it doesn&#x27;t use that account this time.</li>
</ul>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">In Slack channels, threads, and group chats, a Team Bot uses only its owner&#x27;s plugins, and asks before it uses your accounts.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">A connected account always acts as the person the Bot is answering. One teammate&#x27;s connection is never used for someone else.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To make every Team Bot ask again, open <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>General</strong> &gt; <strong>Team Bots</strong> and choose <strong>Clear</strong> next to <strong>Clear connector preferences</strong>.</p>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">In your own chat with a teammate&#x27;s Team Bot, the Bot usually works on your Grok Bot computer. That means it can use the files there and the sites you&#x27;re signed in to there. Sometimes it uses a separate computer just for that conversation, for example when your privacy mode is stricter than the owner&#x27;s.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">In Slack channels, threads, and group chats, a Team Bot uses one shared computer of its own.</p>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">When you chat with a teammate&#x27;s Team Bot, the chat uses your Grok Bot usage, not the owner&#x27;s. <p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">A Team Bot asks for approval only in its owner&#x27;s own chat with it. In teammates&#x27; chats, in Slack, and in group chats, nobody who can answer an approval card is there. So the Bot works within the permissions its owner set up, without stopping to ask. It still asks before it uses your personal accounts.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">If your Enterprise admin enforces Auto-review, Auto-review still checks actions in those chats. <h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-add-a-team-bot-to-slack"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-add-a-team-bot-to-slack" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-add-a-team-bot-to-slack" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-add-a-team-bot-to-slack"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I add a Team Bot to Slack?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">The owner adds it, after publishing:</p>

<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-edit-unpublish-or-delete-a-team-bot"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-edit-unpublish-or-delete-a-team-bot" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-edit-unpublish-or-delete-a-team-bot" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-edit-unpublish-or-delete-a-team-bot"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I edit, unpublish, or delete a Team Bot?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Only the owner can change a Team Bot. Click the Bot&#x27;s name at the top of the chat to open its details:</p>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Plugins</strong>, <strong>Secrets</strong>, <strong>Skills</strong>, and <strong>Files</strong> hold what the Bot uses for everyone.</li>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To rename it, you can also right-click the Bot in the sidebar and choose <strong>Rename Bot</strong>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To delete it, right-click the Bot in the sidebar and choose <strong>Delete</strong>. This deletes the Bot for your whole team. Teammates lose it, along with their chats and routines on it. It can&#x27;t be undone.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-remove-a-team-bot-from-my-sidebar"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-remove-a-team-bot-from-my-sidebar" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-remove-a-team-bot-from-my-sidebar" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-remove-a-team-bot-from-my-sidebar"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I remove a Team Bot from my sidebar?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Right-click the Bot and choose <strong>Hide from sidebar</strong>. To add it back, choose <strong>New</strong>, then <strong>Team Bots</strong>, and pick it again.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">If your admin added the Bot to your sidebar for you, <strong>Hide from sidebar</strong> isn&#x27;t in its menu, and you can&#x27;t remove it.</p>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">You can&#x27;t start a voice call with a teammate&#x27;s Team Bot.</p>
