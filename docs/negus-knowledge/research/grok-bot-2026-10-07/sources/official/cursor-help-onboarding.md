<ol class="mb-4 prose-ol-markers" style="list-style-type:decimal;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Open Grok Bot and choose <strong>Sign in</strong>. Finish signing in with your Cursor account in your browser. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/sign-in">Sign in to Grok Bot</a>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Click through the short intro.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Grok Bot sets up your first Bot, named <strong>Grok Bot</strong>, and opens a chat with it.</li>
</ol>

<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-create-another-bot"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-create-another-bot" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-create-another-bot" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-create-another-bot"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I create another Bot?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<ol class="mb-4 prose-ol-markers" style="list-style-type:decimal;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Choose <strong>New</strong> in the sidebar, then <strong>Create new Bot</strong>. Grok Bot adds a Bot named <strong>New Bot</strong> and opens its chat. To name it up front, type the name first, then choose <strong>Create &quot;Name&quot; Bot</strong>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Click the Bot&#x27;s name at the top of the chat, then choose <strong>Bot settings</strong>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Give your Bot a short <strong>Name</strong>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Add a <strong>Label</strong> that describes its main job.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Write a <strong>Description</strong> of the work it should do, the sources it should use, and when it should ask for your approval.</li>
</ol>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">You can change these at any time. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/edit-bot">Change a Bot&#x27;s name, picture, and description</a>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">For example:</p>
<blockquote class="my-4 border-primary border-l-4 pl-4 text-muted-foreground italic">
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Name: Research</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Label: Weekly product researcher</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Description: Research one product topic at a time. Use the web and the documents I share. Summarize the main findings with source links, and ask before changing any external service.</p>
</blockquote>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="what-should-i-ask-my-bot-to-do-first"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#what-should-i-ask-my-bot-to-do-first" data-heading-base-id="what-should-i-ask-my-bot-to-do-first" aria-label="Copy link to what-should-i-ask-my-bot-to-do-first"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">What should I ask my Bot to do first?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Start with a short task that has a clear result and does not change an external service. Attach a document and try:</p>
<blockquote class="my-4 border-primary border-l-4 pl-4 text-muted-foreground italic">
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Summarize this document in five bullets. List every date, decision, and open question in a separate section. Cite the section for each item. Do not change the source file.</p>
</blockquote>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Review the answer and send a follow-up if you want a different format. Your Bot keeps its role and context, so you can return to it for related work.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-give-my-bot-access-to-a-tool"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-give-my-bot-access-to-a-tool" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-give-my-bot-access-to-a-tool" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-give-my-bot-access-to-a-tool"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I give my Bot access to a tool?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Connect a plugin when your Bot needs Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, Notion, or another supported service. Follow the <strong>Connect</strong> card in chat, or open <strong>Plugins</strong> from the sidebar. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/connect-plugins">Connect plugins</a>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">For a website without a plugin, open the Bot&#x27;s computer and sign in yourself when prompted. The Bot does not see the password you enter. Your Bots share the same cloud computer, including its files and browser sessions.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="what-should-i-check-before-my-bot-takes-action"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#what-should-i-check-before-my-bot-takes-action" data-heading-base-id="what-should-i-check-before-my-bot-takes-action" aria-label="Copy link to what-should-i-check-before-my-bot-takes-action"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">What should I check before my Bot takes action?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Read each approval card before you continue. Use <strong>Allow once</strong> while you learn how the Bot handles a workflow. Choose <strong>Deny</strong> when the proposed action is not what you asked for.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Keep passwords and API keys out of chat. Use the secure prompt when the Bot asks for a credential. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/secrets">Store secrets securely</a>.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="what-should-i-do-after-my-first-task"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#what-should-i-do-after-my-first-task" data-heading-base-id="what-should-i-do-after-my-first-task" aria-label="Copy link to what-should-i-do-after-my-first-task"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">What should I do after my first task?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Keep the Bot focused on one type of work. Tell it which parts of the result you want to reuse, such as a report format or a list of sources.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Once the workflow produces the result you expect, you can turn it into a <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/routines">routine</a> or connect more tools.</p>

<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-create-another-bot"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-create-another-bot" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-create-another-bot" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-create-another-bot"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I create another Bot?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<ol class="mb-4 prose-ol-markers" style="list-style-type:decimal;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Choose <strong>New</strong> in the sidebar, then <strong>Create new Bot</strong>. Grok Bot adds a Bot named <strong>New Bot</strong> and opens its chat. To name it up front, type the name first, then choose <strong>Create &quot;Name&quot; Bot</strong>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Click the Bot&#x27;s name at the top of the chat, then choose <strong>Bot settings</strong>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Give your Bot a short <strong>Name</strong>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Add a <strong>Label</strong> that describes its main job.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Write a <strong>Description</strong> of the work it should do, the sources it should use, and when it should ask for your approval.</li>
</ol>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">You can change these at any time. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/edit-bot">Change a Bot&#x27;s name, picture, and description</a>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">For example:</p>
<blockquote class="my-4 border-primary border-l-4 pl-4 text-muted-foreground italic">
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Name: Research</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Label: Weekly product researcher</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Description: Research one product topic at a time. Use the web and the documents I share. Summarize the main findings with source links, and ask before changing any external service.</p>
</blockquote>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="what-should-i-ask-my-bot-to-do-first"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#what-should-i-ask-my-bot-to-do-first" data-heading-base-id="what-should-i-ask-my-bot-to-do-first" aria-label="Copy link to what-should-i-ask-my-bot-to-do-first"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">What should I ask my Bot to do first?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Start with a short task that has a clear result and does not change an external service. Attach a document and try:</p>
<blockquote class="my-4 border-primary border-l-4 pl-4 text-muted-foreground italic">
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Summarize this document in five bullets. List every date, decision, and open question in a separate section. Cite the section for each item. Do not change the source file.</p>
</blockquote>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Review the answer and send a follow-up if you want a different format. Your Bot keeps its role and context, so you can return to it for related work.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-give-my-bot-access-to-a-tool"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-give-my-bot-access-to-a-tool" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-give-my-bot-access-to-a-tool" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-give-my-bot-access-to-a-tool"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I give my Bot access to a tool?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Connect a plugin when your Bot needs Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, Notion, or another supported service. Follow the <strong>Connect</strong> card in chat, or open <strong>Plugins</strong> from the sidebar. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/connect-plugins">Connect plugins</a>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">For a website without a plugin, open the Bot&#x27;s computer and sign in yourself when prompted. The Bot does not see the password you enter. Your Bots share the same cloud computer, including its files and browser sessions.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="what-should-i-check-before-my-bot-takes-action"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#what-should-i-check-before-my-bot-takes-action" data-heading-base-id="what-should-i-check-before-my-bot-takes-action" aria-label="Copy link to what-should-i-check-before-my-bot-takes-action"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">What should I check before my Bot takes action?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Read each approval card before you continue. Use <strong>Allow once</strong> while you learn how the Bot handles a workflow. Choose <strong>Deny</strong> when the proposed action is not what you asked for.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Keep passwords and API keys out of chat. Use the secure prompt when the Bot asks for a credential. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/secrets">Store secrets securely</a>.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="what-should-i-do-after-my-first-task"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#what-should-i-do-after-my-first-task" data-heading-base-id="what-should-i-do-after-my-first-task" aria-label="Copy link to what-should-i-do-after-my-first-task"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">What should I do after my first task?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Keep the Bot focused on one type of work. Tell it which parts of the result you want to reuse, such as a report format or a list of sources.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Once the workflow produces the result you expect, you can turn it into a <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/routines">routine</a> or connect more tools.</p>
