<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-set-up-or-change-a-routine"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-set-up-or-change-a-routine" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-set-up-or-change-a-routine" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-set-up-or-change-a-routine"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I set up or change a routine?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Ask the Bot in chat. Say what it should do and when. For example: &quot;Every weekday at 9:00 AM, summarize new support tickets in this chat.&quot; The Bot creates the routine, and it appears in the Bot&#x27;s routines.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To change a routine on desktop, open it and choose <strong>Edit</strong>. Grok Bot starts a message to the Bot that begins &quot;Edit your routine:&quot; with the routine&#x27;s name. Add what should change, then send it.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">On the phone, open the Bot&#x27;s profile and tap <strong>Add routine</strong> under <strong>Routines</strong>. Grok Bot starts a message that begins &quot;Set up a routine to &quot;. Finish the sentence and send it.</p>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">On desktop:</p>
<ol class="mb-4 prose-ol-markers" style="list-style-type:decimal;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Click the Bot&#x27;s name at the top of the chat to open its details.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Choose <strong>Tasks</strong>. Routines are under <strong>Routines</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Each row shows the routine&#x27;s name and when it runs, or <strong>Paused</strong>. Use the switch on the row to pause or resume it. If the Bot has no routines, you see <strong>Ask in chat to set a routine</strong>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Click a routine to open it:</p>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Instruction</strong> is what the Bot does on each run.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>When to run</strong> lists the schedule and the events that start it.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Webhook</strong> shows the URL and key, for a routine that a webhook call starts.</li>
</ul>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">The buttons at the bottom are <strong>Pause</strong> or <strong>Resume</strong>, <strong>Test</strong>, <strong>Edit</strong>, and <strong>Delete routine</strong> (the trash icon). Deleting asks you to confirm. It stops future runs and can&#x27;t be undone.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">On the phone, open the Bot&#x27;s profile and find <strong>Routines</strong>. Tap a routine to see <strong>Active</strong>, <strong>Schedule</strong>, <strong>Next run</strong>, <strong>Instruction</strong>, and <strong>Run history</strong>. Turn off <strong>Active</strong> to pause the routine. To delete one, swipe the routine&#x27;s row.</p>

<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="what-should-i-change-if-usage-drops-quickly"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#what-should-i-change-if-usage-drops-quickly" data-heading-base-id="what-should-i-change-if-usage-drops-quickly" aria-label="Copy link to what-should-i-change-if-usage-drops-quickly"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">What should I change if usage drops quickly?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Start with how often your routines run. Each run spends usage, so a routine that runs often spends more than one that runs rarely. An hourly schedule, a short interval, or a Slack trigger on a busy channel can use a week of usage in a day.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">If <strong>Weekly usage</strong> is falling faster than you expect, pause routines you don&#x27;t need. Then ask the Bot to widen schedules and narrow Slack triggers before you raise a plan or an on-demand limit. A routine that never runs doesn&#x27;t spend usage. <strong>Test</strong> does, because it performs the work.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/plans">Plans and billing</a> for weekly usage and the on-demand monthly limit.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-slack-triggers-work"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-slack-triggers-work" data-heading-base-id="how-do-slack-triggers-work" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-slack-triggers-work"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do Slack triggers work?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">A Slack trigger is part of a routine, not the Slack plugin. The plugin lets a Bot post and read as the Slack user you connected. A trigger starts the routine when something happens in Slack. Connect Slack on the Cursor account that owns the Bot before you rely on it. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/connect-plugins#what-can-a-plugin-do">Connect plugins</a>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Ask the Bot for the trigger you want. A routine can start when the Bot is mentioned, when a word or phrase is mentioned, when you react to a message, or on any message. It can watch one channel or all of Slack. <strong>When to run</strong> shows the trigger in words, such as a phrase and a channel.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Keep the channel and phrase narrow. A trigger on any message in a busy channel runs often and spends usage on messages that don&#x27;t need the Bot. Only new activity counts. Messages from before the trigger existed don&#x27;t start a run.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="where-do-the-webhook-url-and-key-live-and-what-does-a-200-mean"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#where-do-the-webhook-url-and-key-live-and-what-does-a-200-mean" data-heading-base-id="where-do-the-webhook-url-and-key-live-and-what-does-a-200-mean" aria-label="Copy link to where-do-the-webhook-url-and-key-live-and-what-does-a-200-mean"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">Where do the webhook URL and key live, and what does a 200 mean?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Ask the Bot to add a webhook trigger to the routine. Then open the routine on desktop. The <strong>Webhook</strong> section shows three fields:</p>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>POST to</strong> is the URL.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>key</strong> is the secret. Senders include it as <code class="rounded-sm border border-border bg-card/80 p-1 font-mono text-xs">Authorization: Bearer &lt;key&gt;</code>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>header</strong> is that full header, ready to copy.</li>
</ul>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Click a field to copy it. Send an HTTP POST to that URL with the header. You can send a JSON body. The Bot receives that body with the routine instruction.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">A response of <strong>200</strong> means Grok Bot accepted the call and started a run. It does not mean the Bot has finished the instruction. Check the Bot&#x27;s chat for the result. Any other response means a run did not start. Confirm the routine isn&#x27;t paused and that the request uses the current key.</p>

It can run on a schedule, or when something happens, such as a Slack message, a GitHub, Linear, Sentry, or PagerDuty event, an email, or a webhook call. Routines run in the cloud while your laptop is closed.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-set-up-or-change-a-routine"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-set-up-or-change-a-routine" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-set-up-or-change-a-routine" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-set-up-or-change-a-routine"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I set up or change a routine?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Ask the Bot in chat. Say what it should do and when. For example: &quot;Every weekday at 9:00 AM, summarize new support tickets in this chat.&quot; The Bot creates the routine, and it appears in the Bot&#x27;s routines.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">To change a routine on desktop, open it and choose <strong>Edit</strong>. Grok Bot starts a message to the Bot that begins &quot;Edit your routine:&quot; with the routine&#x27;s name. Add what should change, then send it.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">On the phone, open the Bot&#x27;s profile and tap <strong>Add routine</strong> under <strong>Routines</strong>. Grok Bot starts a message that begins &quot;Set up a routine to &quot;. Finish the sentence and send it.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="where-do-i-see-a-bots-routines"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#where-do-i-see-a-bots-routines" data-heading-base-id="where-do-i-see-a-bots-routines" aria-label="Copy link to where-do-i-see-a-bots-routines"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">Where do I see a Bot&#x27;s routines?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">On desktop:</p>
<ol class="mb-4 prose-ol-markers" style="list-style-type:decimal;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Click the Bot&#x27;s name at the top of the chat to open its details.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px">Choose <strong>Tasks</strong>. Routines are under <strong>Routines</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Each row shows the routine&#x27;s name and when it runs, or <strong>Paused</strong>. Use the switch on the row to pause or resume it. If the Bot has no routines, you see <strong>Ask in chat to set a routine</strong>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Click a routine to open it:</p>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Instruction</strong> is what the Bot does on each run.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>When to run</strong> lists the schedule and the events that start it.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>Webhook</strong> shows the URL and key, for a routine that a webhook call starts.</li>
</ul>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">The buttons at the bottom are <strong>Pause</strong> or <strong>Resume</strong>, <strong>Test</strong>, <strong>Edit</strong>, and <strong>Delete routine</strong> (the trash icon). Deleting asks you to confirm. It stops future runs and can&#x27;t be undone.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">On the phone, open the Bot&#x27;s profile and find <strong>Routines</strong>. Tap a routine to see <strong>Active</strong>, <strong>Schedule</strong>, <strong>Next run</strong>, <strong>Instruction</strong>, and <strong>Run history</strong>. Turn off <strong>Active</strong> to pause the routine. To delete one, swipe the routine&#x27;s row.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-schedules-and-time-zones-work"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-schedules-and-time-zones-work" data-heading-base-id="how-do-schedules-and-time-zones-work" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-schedules-and-time-zones-work"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do schedules and time zones work?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Schedules use the time zone in <strong>Settings</strong>, under <strong>Bot</strong>, labeled <strong>Timezone</strong>. <strong>Auto-detect</strong> follows this computer. Pick a zone when the routine should follow a different one, such as the office where the work belongs.</p>

<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Ask for a schedule in plain words, like &quot;every weekday at 8:00 AM&quot; or &quot;every 2 hours&quot;. Then open the routine and check <strong>When to run</strong>. It shows the saved schedule in words, so you can confirm it matches what you meant. If it doesn&#x27;t, choose <strong>Edit</strong> and tell the Bot what to fix.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">A new routine waits for its next scheduled time. It doesn&#x27;t run when it&#x27;s created. If you set up &quot;every day at 8:00 AM&quot; at 8:05, the first run is tomorrow at 8:00 AM. To try it right away, use <strong>Test</strong>.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-i-test-a-routine"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-i-test-a-routine" data-heading-base-id="how-do-i-test-a-routine" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-i-test-a-routine"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do I test a routine?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Open the routine on desktop and choose <strong>Test</strong>. The button says <strong>Running…</strong> until the run finishes. The result shows up in the Bot&#x27;s chat.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">A test does real work. It can change files and use connected plugins. Use a safe input. If the test can&#x27;t start, you see <strong>Couldn&#x27;t start a test run</strong>, or a message that explains why.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Some routines don&#x27;t show <strong>Test</strong>. They still run on their schedule or trigger.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="what-should-i-change-if-usage-drops-quickly"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#what-should-i-change-if-usage-drops-quickly" data-heading-base-id="what-should-i-change-if-usage-drops-quickly" aria-label="Copy link to what-should-i-change-if-usage-drops-quickly"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">What should I change if usage drops quickly?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Start with how often your routines run. Each run spends usage, so a routine that runs often spends more than one that runs rarely. An hourly schedule, a short interval, or a Slack trigger on a busy channel can use a week of usage in a day.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">If <strong>Weekly usage</strong> is falling faster than you expect, pause routines you don&#x27;t need. Then ask the Bot to widen schedules and narrow Slack triggers before you raise a plan or an on-demand limit. A routine that never runs doesn&#x27;t spend usage. <strong>Test</strong> does, because it performs the work.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/plans">Plans and billing</a> for weekly usage and the on-demand monthly limit.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="how-do-slack-triggers-work"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#how-do-slack-triggers-work" data-heading-base-id="how-do-slack-triggers-work" aria-label="Copy link to how-do-slack-triggers-work"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">How do Slack triggers work?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">A Slack trigger is part of a routine, not the Slack plugin. The plugin lets a Bot post and read as the Slack user you connected. A trigger starts the routine when something happens in Slack. Connect Slack on the Cursor account that owns the Bot before you rely on it. See <a class="text-foreground underline decoration-neutral-600 underline-offset-2 transition-colors hover:decoration-neutral-700" href="/help/grok-bot/connect-plugins#what-can-a-plugin-do">Connect plugins</a>.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Ask the Bot for the trigger you want. A routine can start when the Bot is mentioned, when a word or phrase is mentioned, when you react to a message, or on any message. It can watch one channel or all of Slack. <strong>When to run</strong> shows the trigger in words, such as a phrase and a channel.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Keep the channel and phrase narrow. A trigger on any message in a busy channel runs often and spends usage on messages that don&#x27;t need the Bot. Only new activity counts. Messages from before the trigger existed don&#x27;t start a run.</p>
<h2 class="text-medium mt-12 mb-4" id="where-do-the-webhook-url-and-key-live-and-what-does-a-200-mean"><a class="group flex items-center no-underline hover:no-underline" href="#where-do-the-webhook-url-and-key-live-and-what-does-a-200-mean" data-heading-base-id="where-do-the-webhook-url-and-key-live-and-what-does-a-200-mean" aria-label="Copy link to where-do-the-webhook-url-and-key-live-and-what-does-a-200-mean"><span data-heading-highlight-target="true">Where do the webhook URL and key live, and what does a 200 mean?</span><span class="ml-2 text-muted-foreground opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100"><div class="relative size-3" aria-hidden="true"><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-copy absolute inset-0 size-3 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-100 scale-100" aria-hidden="true"><rect width="14" height="14" x="8" y="8" rx="2" ry="2"></rect><path d="M4 16c-1.1 0-2-.9-2-2V4c0-1.1.9-2 2-2h10c1.1 0 2 .9 2 2"></path></svg><svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" width="24" height="24" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="2" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" class="lucide lucide-check absolute inset-0 size-3 text-green-600 transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out opacity-0 scale-50" aria-hidden="true"><path d="M20 6 9 17l-5-5"></path></svg></div></span></a></h2>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Ask the Bot to add a webhook trigger to the routine. Then open the routine on desktop. The <strong>Webhook</strong> section shows three fields:</p>
<ul class="my-6 prose-ul-markers" style="list-style-type:square;list-style-position:outside;padding-left:calc(4rem / 3);line-height:1.5">
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>POST to</strong> is the URL.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>key</strong> is the secret. Senders include it as <code class="rounded-sm border border-border bg-card/80 p-1 font-mono text-xs">Authorization: Bearer &lt;key&gt;</code>.</li>
<li style="padding-inline-start:4px;margin:0 0 0.5rem 0;font-size:15px"><strong>header</strong> is that full header, ready to copy.</li>
</ul>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">Click a field to copy it. Send an HTTP POST to that URL with the header. You can send a JSON body. The Bot receives that body with the routine instruction.</p>
<p class="text-base mb-4 leading-relaxed!">A response of <strong>200</strong> means Grok Bot accepted the call and started a run. It does not mean the Bot has finished the instruction. Check the Bot&#x27;s chat for the result. Any other response means a run did not start. Confirm the routine isn&#x27;t paused and that the request uses the current key.</p>
